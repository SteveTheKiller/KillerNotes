KillerNotes MCP

Find the right note from your agent.

What it does

KillerMCP gives your agent a read-only KillerNotes search tool. Ask for notes by title, tag, or text and get matching snippets back without opening the app yourself.

killer find my notes about the office network

The search reads your local notes database. Encrypted databases cannot currently be unlocked through MCP.

One connection for the family

KillerNotes is part of KillerMCP, the shared connection for KillerTools and the Killer apps. You install KillerMCP once. It finds supported apps on your computer and makes their tools available to your agent.

The installer and KillerNotes integration are still in development. Setup instructions and a download link will appear here when they are ready for release.
